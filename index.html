Privacy Policy for Projecto: Universal IR Remote

Effective Date: April 26, 2026

Smart Remote Labs ("we," "us," or "our") operates the Projecto: Universal IR Remote mobile application (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you use our App.

1. Information Collection and Use

We use information solely to provide and improve the App. By using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy.

A. Personal Data

We do not require you to provide personally identifiable information (such as your name, email address, phone number, or physical address) to use the core remote control features of the App.

B. Device Permissions

To function correctly, the App may request access to certain device features:

  • Infrared (IR): Used solely to transmit remote control signals to compatible projectors. No IR signals are recorded, stored, or transmitted to our servers.
  • Vibration: Used to provide haptic feedback when buttons are pressed.
  • Internet Access: Used to load advertisements and enable analytics and crash reporting services.

2. Third-Party Services

We use trusted third-party services to support advertising, analytics, and crash reporting. These providers may collect anonymized or device-level data in accordance with their own privacy policies.

Google AdMob

We use Google AdMob to display advertisements within the App. Google may use device identifiers, including the Android Advertising ID, to provide personalized or non-personalized ads.

Learn more about how Google uses data here:

https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Google Firebase (Analytics & Crashlytics)

We use Google Firebase services to improve app performance and stability:

  • Firebase Analytics: Collects anonymized usage information such as session duration and feature interactions.
  • Firebase Crashlytics: Collects anonymized crash logs, device model, OS version, and diagnostic data when the app crashes.

Firebase Privacy Policy:

https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

3. Data Safety & Retention

We do not sell, rent, or trade user data to third parties.

Analytics and diagnostic information collected via third-party services is retained only as long as necessary to improve application performance, security, and user experience.

4. Children’s Privacy

The App is not directed toward children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.

5. Legal Basis (If Applicable)

If you are located in jurisdictions requiring a legal basis for data processing (such as the European Economic Area), our legal basis for processing anonymized analytics and advertising data is legitimate interest in improving our services and providing free access to the App through advertising.
